Our Platform

Restoring Economic Stability

We will implement sound fiscal policies that foster growth and ensure prosperity for all Capricans. We are committed to rebuilding the middle class through equitable taxation and massive investments in green technology and infrastructure.

Unified Security and Diplomacy

Caprica must lead on the global stage with a unified voice. We will rebuild our international alliances and ensure our intelligence and security apparatus operates with both efficiency and profound respect for civil liberties.

Environmental Stewardship

Climate action is an absolute economic imperative. Under the leadership of Adriana Flash, we will transition Caprica to a fully sustainable energy grid, creating millions of jobs and securing our environment for future generations.

Healthcare as a Right

Every Caprican deserves access to world-class medical care. We will expand public health infrastructure, reduce prescription costs, and ensure that preventative care is universally available across all sectors.
