CAMPAIGN 2068: TROTTIER / FLASH
BRAND IDENTITY & UI/UX DESIGN SPECIFICATION
CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL CAMPAIGN USE ONLY

CORE STRATEGY & PSYCHOLOGICAL DIRECTIVE

The objective of this digital presence is to project stability, competence, and a unified front. The user must feel that Mandy Trottier and Adriana Flash represent a return to serious, effective governance. The design rejects the previous cycle's aggression, utilizing a "Modern Editorial" aesthetic characterized by stately typography, clean grid structures, and ample white space to project authority, transparency, and optimism. 

COLOR PALETTE

The palette relies on clean white space, using specific party colors for targeted emphasis.

PRIMARY BACKGROUND: Crisp White (Hex: #FFFFFF) & Off-White (Hex: #FAFAFA)
Rationale: Creates a clean, open, and transparent environment.

PRIMARY TEXT: Deep Charcoal (Hex: #1A1A1A)
Rationale: Provides high contrast for legibility without the harshness of pure black.

PRIMARY ACCENTS:
- ALP Red: #BF2519 (Used for primary navigation, primary buttons, and Trottier-specific elements)
- DGC Green: #0d8f3d (Used for secondary accents and Flash-specific elements)

COALITION ACCENTS (Used exclusively in the "Endorsed By" section):
- SDA Red: #D7003A
- MR Blue: #0f2af7

TYPOGRAPHY

Typography acts as the foundation of our credibility, relying on traditional editorial hierarchy.

HEADLINES & SLOGANS: Merriweather (Google Fonts)
Style: Stately, highly legible serif.
Usage: Standard capitalization.
Rationale: Feels authoritative, historical, and deeply serious. It evokes prestige journalism and official documentation.

BODY & UI TEXT: Inter (Google Fonts)
Style: Clean, geometric sans-serif.
Usage: Standard capitalization, excellent legibility across all devices.
Rationale: Provides a modern, accessible counterpoint to the serif headings.

MOTION & ANIMATION PROFILES

Movement on the site must be subtle, smooth, and purposeful.
- Fade-ins on scroll to reveal content gracefully.
- Soft transition on hover states (buttons slightly lifting or colors smoothly shifting).
- Complete removal of jarring, mechanical, or flashing animations.

IMAGERY & COMPOSITION

PORTRAITURE
Treatment: Full color, bright, and optimistic. 
Angle: Eye-level, engaging directly with the viewer or caught in candid moments of collaborative work.
Expression: Warm, resolute, and approachable.

UI / UX ARCHITECTURE

BORDERS: Thin, elegant dividers (1px solid #EAEAEA) separate sections. Use soft drop shadows for elevated elements like cards.
NAVIGATION: Clear, standard terminology ("Our Platform", "Meet the Candidates", "Join Us").
THE CTA: Optimistic and inviting ("Join the Campaign", "Volunteer Today").

COPYWRITING RULES (STRICT ENFORCEMENT)
- No em dashes (—) under any circumstances.
- Absolutely avoid structures like "is not X, but Y". State the affirmative directly and forcefully.
